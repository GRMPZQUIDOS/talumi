# Documented cases

Real translation errors with public sources. Each case shows the original, the published translation and what changed.

## 1. Insulin pump manual, German edition (2010)

An official field safety notice sent to the German federal institute for drugs and medical devices (BfArM) reports a translation error in the German manual of an insulin pump, chapter 5, page 71. The same warning was translated correctly on page 73 of the same manual. All other languages were correct.

| | German text |
|---|---|
| Correct (page 73) | Vergewissern Sie sich, dass das Infusionsset von Ihrem Körper **getrennt** ist, bevor Sie einen Rücklauf durchführen oder den Schlauch des Infusionssets füllen. |
| Published (page 71) | Vergewissern Sie sich, dass das Infusionsset mit Ihrem Körper **verbunden** ist, bevor Sie einen Rücklauf durchführen oder den Schlauch des Infusionssets füllen. |

*Disconnected* became *connected*. Followed to the letter, the instruction could lead to an unintended insulin delivery.

Source: field safety notice, December 2010, [BfArM](https://www.bfarm.de/SharedDocs/Kundeninfos/DE/07/2011/04749-10_kundeninfo_de.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)

## 2. Proust, English title (1922–1992)

*À la recherche du temps perdu* was published in English in 1922 as *Remembrance of Things Past*, a line from Shakespeare's sonnet 30. *Perdu* means both lost and wasted, a double meaning that carries the novel. The English title kept only the memory. It was replaced by *In Search of Lost Time* in 1992.

Sources: [Public Domain Review](https://publicdomainreview.org/essay/lost-in-translation-proust-and-scott-moncrieff/), [Project MUSE](https://muse.jhu.edu/article/647832/pdf)

## 3. Legionella urine test, German instructions (2016)

A field safety notice for a urine antigen test lists errors limited to the German instructions: the test step asked for drops of the positive or negative control instead of the patient's urine sample, a negation disappeared ("concentrated" samples instead of "non-concentrated"), and "shadow line" became "black line".

Source: field safety notice, 30 March 2016, [BfArM](https://www.bfarm.de/SharedDocs/Kundeninfos/DE/08/2016/02180-16_kundeninfo_de.pdf?__blob=publicationFile)

## 4. Allergen labels (EU rapid alert system for food, RASFF)

- **2022.3085**: hazelnut flour. The Dutch word *noten* (nuts) was translated into French as *noix de cajou* (cashew nuts).
- **2020.6104**: chocolate bar. The allergens in the Italian translation did not match the original Romanian and Hungarian labelling. Consumer recall, risk classified as serious.

Source: [RASFF Window](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/rasff-window/screen/search)
