# Talumi trademark policy

The Talumi code is free software under the GNU AGPL v3. The **Talumi name, the logo, the forms तलुमि · タルミ · 塔鲁米 and the Talumi certificate** are not covered by that license. They identify the official Talumi service and the translators it certifies.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software or service is "based on Talumi" or "uses the Talumi engine".
- Refer to Talumi in articles, talks, teaching and comparisons.
- Use the name in a fork's documentation to explain where it comes from.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a product, service, company or domain "Talumi", or anything confusingly similar.
- Use the Talumi logo.
- Present a translation as "Talumi certified", or issue any document that looks like a Talumi certificate.
- Suggest that you are endorsed by, partnered with or part of Talumi.

## Certificates

Only the Talumi service issues Talumi certificates, and only for translators listed in the Talumi registry. Every genuine certificate can be verified on the Talumi website. A certificate that cannot be verified there is not a Talumi certificate.

For permission or partnership requests, open an issue titled "Trademark request".
